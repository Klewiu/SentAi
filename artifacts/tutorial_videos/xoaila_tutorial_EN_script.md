## Xoaila — a company profile AI can read

Meet Xoaila. In the next few minutes, you will see how to publish a trustworthy multilingual company profile for people, search engines and AI systems.

## What does Xoaila do?

Xoaila organizes company information and publishes it at stable web addresses. Customers get a clear profile, while search engines, data aggregators, AI models and AI agents can retrieve the same facts as JSON, JSON-LD, Markdown and llms.txt. This supports indexing and improves the chance that AI systems understand and cite the company's offering accurately.

## From account to publication

The workflow has six clear steps: register, choose a plan, add a company page, complete verification, generate the data and publish. Verification helps prevent impersonation and makes the catalog more trustworthy.

## Choose the right profile scope

Basic includes one company and one language. Plus adds a second profile, a second language, specialties, frequently asked questions and official social profiles. Pro supports up to three profiles and languages, plus a product and service catalog. Every plan includes all four data formats.

## Create your account

Enter a username, company name, email address, country and a secure password, then verify your email. If Google sign-in is configured, you can also use your Google account.

## Your customer dashboard

The dashboard shows the publication stage, your profile allowance and every company you manage. With an active plan and completed verification, you can open each saved language version and its public machine-readable formats.

## 1. Company details

Start with facts that identify the business. Enter the official company name, website and business type. You can also add a public email address, telephone number and location. Use details customers can verify on your own website.

## 2. Languages and descriptions

Select the languages included in your plan and choose the main profile language. The Editing language selector opens separate fields for each translation. Xoaila does not translate your content automatically, so every version preserves the wording approved by your company.

## Specialties, products and services

Add specific specialties as short phrases. On Pro, every product or service has its own name, concise description and optional source page. Keep items in the same order across translations so the language versions remain correctly connected.

## 3. FAQs and official profiles

Use FAQs for real customer questions about scope, timing, service area or ordering. Keep each answer precise, and add a supporting link when the fact can be confirmed on your website. You can also list official social profiles, one web address per line.

## The public company profile

After payment and verification, the public profile becomes available. Visitors can switch language versions and see company facts, specialties, products, FAQs and sources in the selected language. Editing content does not cancel an existing payment or verification.

## Structured data ready to retrieve

The same information is published as structured JSON and Schema.org JSON-LD. Each language version declares its language, uses stable identifiers and links to the other formats. Search engines, data aggregators, AI models and AI agents can retrieve facts without guessing the structure of an HTML page.

## Formats for agents and language models

Markdown provides an easy-to-read narrative, while llms.txt points agents toward the most relevant resources. Search engines, data aggregators, AI models and AI agents can use stable addresses and retrieve current data. A complete, current profile backed by sources provides the strongest value.

## Publish a trustworthy company profile

Create an account, choose a plan and describe your business in your own words. Xoaila turns that information into a consistent public profile and AI-friendly formats. Update it whenever your offering, location or contact details change.
